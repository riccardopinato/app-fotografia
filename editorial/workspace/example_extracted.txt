Reflective meters interpret the brightness of the framed scene rather than identifying the physical material in front of the lens. A useful field check is to compare the meter suggestion with the visual intention and preserve the highlight or shadow detail that matters most.

Exposure compensation changes the target chosen by an automatic or semi-automatic exposure mode. It does not repair an unsuitable metering area, so the photographer should first decide what subject tone is being measured and then make one deliberate adjustment.
